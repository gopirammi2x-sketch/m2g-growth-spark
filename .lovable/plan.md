# Expand M2G to Five Business Verticals

## Goal
Preserve the existing one-page website and its current brand feel while expanding the services presentation from three to five clear business verticals. Keep the page concise by showing essential information first and placing detailed sub-services in compact expandable areas.

## What will change

### 1. Five-service navigation and overview
- Keep the existing header, but make **Services** open a compact menu with links to all five verticals on desktop and mobile.
- Retain smooth scrolling and use the requested anchors: `digital-marketing`, `ai-advertising`, `automation`, `ai-business-services`, and `ecommerce-solutions`.
- Replace the current services introduction with:
  - **Five Practical Solutions. One Business Growth Partner.**
  - The supplied supporting copy.
- Add a concise five-card overview containing number, title, tagline, description, visual/icon, and **Explore Service →** action.

### 2. Detailed service sections
- Refresh the first three existing service sections with the supplied taglines, descriptions, features, and simple visual-flow labels.
- Add **AI-Powered Business Services** as vertical 04 with one strong visual and a compact expandable grid for its 10 sub-services.
- Add **E-Commerce Solutions** as vertical 05 with one strong journey visual and a compact visual grid for its 8 sub-services.
- Keep the service detail hierarchy clear so the homepage does not feel like a long catalogue.

### 3. Premium visual system
- Keep the existing three premium service images and generate matching new imagery for AI Business Services and E-Commerce.
- Generate coordinated smaller e-commerce visuals for website design, online store, marketplace setup, store management, product content, social advertising, short-form video advertising, and automation.
- Use realistic commercial imagery with navy, green, beige, and restrained gold accents; avoid copied interfaces, brand marks, fake metrics, cartoons, and watermarks.
- Convert generated imagery to optimized WebP and lazy-load images below the first screen.

### 4. Brand and supporting copy
- Align global brand tokens to the supplied palette: navy `#12213B`, warm beige `#F8F0DF`, white, M2G green from the logo, and gold `#C99318`.
- Preserve navy as the dominant color and green for primary actions; use gold only for selective premium accents.
- Update the hero/service positioning and credibility text only where needed to communicate **Digital + AI + Automation + E-Commerce**.
- Update the About statistic from three to five core verticals.
- Update the contact-service selector and footer service list to include all five verticals while preserving the working WhatsApp enquiry flow and QR codes.

### 5. Industries and service CTA
- Keep the existing ten industry images and card design.
- Update the Industries supporting text to the supplied five-vertical message while retaining concise card descriptions.
- Replace the existing closing statement in Services with the supplied **Which Part of Your Business Do You Want to Grow?** CTA, including working consultation and WhatsApp buttons.

## Validation
- Verify all five service links scroll to the correct section.
- Verify expandable sub-services are keyboard-accessible and readable without overwhelming the page.
- Verify the consultation form still opens the encoded WhatsApp message and includes all five services.
- Check desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts for overflow, text collisions, image framing, menu behavior, animations, and console errors.
- Leave the project in Preview only; do not publish.
